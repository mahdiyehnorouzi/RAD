Scope: `/passport/[code]`, one work's passport. Mode: Read (the visitor understands one work's life and can act on it).

## Direction contract

THESIS: The page is the passport itself: a torn paper document laid over the work's photograph, not a stack of headed text lists.

OWN-WORLD: The trace world (cream page, lighter paper sheets with soft offset shadow, pale deckle under every torn edge, oxide thread and ink, kiln-green actions, IRANYekanX bold titles, Aref Ruqaa only for notes). Stops on the work's trail are inked entry stamps; care is a hanging paper tag.

STORY: The visitor sees the work, reads its record, scrubs from idea to RAD, sees the making prints and the unexpected change, sees where it lives now, then views the work, the museum record, or copies the link.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Phone: photo full width, sheet torn over its foot with a peeled corner at the reading end; eyebrow, bold title, one-line lede at the start, tilted oxide seal and fingerprint at the end, ledger rows below. Desktop: sheet at reading start, photo bleeding off the reading end, a vertical tear between.

FORM: The user's approved phone mockup (screenshot), extended to the rest of the page; no seed (brief-pinned).

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
