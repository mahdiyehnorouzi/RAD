# Product

## Platform

web

## Users

RAD serves people in Iran and abroad who want to discover or commission one-of-one art and design objects. They may arrive with a specific reference image, a remembered form, a colour, an unusual detail, or only a feeling, and want to see what that source can become in physical form.

## Product Purpose

RAD makes singular physical works and gives each work a legible story. It connects an initial idea with a maker and a material, preserving the changes that happen between intention and the finished object. Success means a visitor understands why the result is not an exact copy, trusts the person and process behind it, and can either acquire an available work or begin their own RAD.

## Positioning

RAD is not a made-to-order reproduction service or a mass-production catalogue. An input passes through a person, a maker, and a material; the differences introduced by that passage are part of the work. Because the same path cannot happen twice, each finished RAD is one of one.

## Operating Context

Visitors can browse available works, start a custom design from an image or idea, follow the making process, and view records that compare the original description, an imagined version, the artist's interpretation, and the material result.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Persian is the default language and English is fully supported.
- Internal links preserve the selected language.
- RAD spans ceramics, painting, textile, woodwork, sculpture, jewellery, and hand printing; it must not be described as a ceramics-only brand.
- Every work has one permanent `RĀD / NNN` archive number and a `1 / 1` identifier.
- Product and process claims must come from real repository content; no founder biography, customer proof, or production claim may be invented.

## Brand Commitments

The name رَد means what remains after something passes: the trace of hand, material, accident, and maker. The voice is direct, human, quiet, and specific. RAD avoids corporate mission/vision/value language and speaks about ideas, passage, material, difference, and a single result.

## Product Principles

- Show the trace of transformation instead of promising exact reproduction.
- Treat variation as evidence of making, not as a defect to hide.
- Keep every work singular, numbered, and connected to its record.
- Let visitors meet the human process before asking them to trust it.
- End brand storytelling with a clear path into available works or a personal commission.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

The storefront must remain keyboard accessible, respect reduced-motion preferences, maintain readable contrast, and preserve correct RTL/LTR behavior across Persian and English.
